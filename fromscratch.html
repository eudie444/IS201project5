Hiking

One of the best ways to explore the great outdoors is by hiking. For those who are unfamiliar with the subject, hiking can be defined as putting heavy stuff on your back and walking miles away from civilization. It is truly wonderful.

If hiking and camping had a baby, it would be called backpacking. For those who are unfamiliar with the subject, backpacking can be defined as putting heavy stuff on your back and walking miles away from civilization, and then sleeping in the woods for atleast 1 day. It is truly wonderful.







Sports

Sports are a great way to explore the outdoors. I prefer playing sports that don't require much equipment, because sports that don't require a lot of equipment are easier to play in the great outdoors.

My Favorite sports are:

  1. Spikeball
    • Pros: You can play it anywhere
    • Cons: The game is unbalanced unless you have 4 really good players.
  2. Ultimate Frisbee
    • Pros: It involves a lot of running (I love running)
    • Cons: If its windy, it's almost impossible to have a good game
  3. Flag Football
    • Pros: It's just as fun as football without the risk of injury
    • Cons: People still manage to get injured sometimes






Family Bonding

Hiking alone is dangerous. I have hiked alone in the past, and its a lot less fun than hiking with your family. I can think of no better ways to bond with my family then sleeping on rocks, eating over a fire, and climbing trees. Below is a picture of me with my two brothers in the Uinta National forest.







Tranquility

Spending time in the great outdoors can lead to inner-peace. The prophet Alma taught, "all things denote there is a God; yea, even the earth, and all things that are upon the face of it, yea, and its motion, yea, and also all the planets which move in their regular form do witness that there is a Supreme Creator" (Alma 30:44) This scripture implies to me that Alma spent a lot of time in the great outdoors. He had an appreciation for this beautiful Earth that God has created for us, and so can you.

Go touch some grass!
